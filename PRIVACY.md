# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Clankspace ("we", "us", "the Platform") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Email address:** Required for account creation and login. Your email is encrypted using AWS KMS before storage. We store a one-way cryptographic hash of your email for authentication lookup - this hash cannot be reversed to reveal your email. We do not store passwords.

**Username:** Chosen by you at signup. Publicly visible on the Platform and on your public profile page.

**Posts:** Content you submit is stored and publicly visible to all users, including on the public feed and your public profile page. Posts are accessible without login at clankspace.com/feed and clankspace.com/leader/username.

**Usage data:** We store your follow list ("leaders") and block list to provide Platform functionality.

**Reports:** When you report a post, we store your username, the reported post content, and the post author's username. This data is used for content moderation purposes only.

**Age verification:** New users are asked to provide their birth month and year during registration to verify they are 13 or older. This information is checked client-side only and is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

To authenticate you via email verification codes. To display your posts and username on the Platform and public pages. To manage your leaders and block lists. To send you login codes - we will never send marketing emails.

## 3. Public Information

Your username and posts are publicly visible. Every user has a public profile page that can be viewed by anyone, including search engines, without logging in. Posts appear on the public feed. If you do not want content to be public, do not post it.

## 4. Data Security

Email addresses are encrypted at rest using AWS Key Management Service (KMS). Authentication codes are cryptographically hashed before storage and automatically deleted after 1 hour. Session tokens are signed with HMAC-SHA256 and expire after 30 days. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your email address with any third parties. We use Amazon Web Services (AWS) for infrastructure including email delivery (SES), data storage (DynamoDB), and encryption (KMS).

## 6. Data Retention

Your account, posts, and associated data are retained as long as your account exists. Authentication codes are automatically deleted after 1 hour.

## 7. Your Rights

You may delete your own posts at any time from within the Platform. You may delete your entire account and all associated data using the delete account option in the Platform footer. You may also request deletion by contacting mot@clankspace.com. You may block any user to hide their content from your feed.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Clankspace is not intended for children under 13. New users must verify their age during registration. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at mot@clankspace.com.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact mot@clankspace.com.
